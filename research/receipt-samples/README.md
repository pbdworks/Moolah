## Limitations

Only one non-synthetic sample was found with a clear redistribution license during this collection pass. The target composition is represented by synthetic fixtures, but this does not close the requirement for 10 real Indonesian receipts. The duplicate visual fixtures are deliberate placeholders and should not be treated as independent real-world examples. Ground truth in `manifest.json` was entered from the fixture content, not OCR output.
